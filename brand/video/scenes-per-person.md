# SiteBakkie AI scenes: every scene for every person (phone seen from behind)

New rule in every prompt: the phone screen is never visible. The camera sees the back or the side of the phone;
the person's face and actions tell the story. Cut to the real app screens (sitebakkie.mp4, slides/ or long screens/)
between shots: after Scene 2 (say the day → task sheet), after Scene 3 (reaction → site board), and after Scene 7 (inspector → safety file).

Each prompt is complete: paste one block per clip and attach that person's reference image.
Scenes 6a, 6b, 6c and 8 have no lead person; they are the same for everyone (at the end).
Before you use a take, check it: no visible phone screen; hard hat, vest and boots on everyone; nobody in an open trench; guardrails on scaffolds.

**Suggested edit order:** 1 → app title card → 2 → app: say the day + task sheet → 3 → app: site board → 4 → 5 → 6a/6b/6c → 7 → app: safety file → 8 → app end card.
