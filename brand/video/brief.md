# SiteBakkie promo: voice-over script and AI site scenes

The app video (`sitebakkie.mp4`, 34 s, 9:16) is the spine. This brief gives:

1. **Voice-over A**: timed to the app video as it is (34 s).
2. **Voice-over B**: for the longer cut with site scenes (about 50 s).
3. **AI video prompts**: 8 site scenes to cut between the app screens.

The one idea to land: **one voice note in the morning builds the whole day's safety file.**

## 3. AI video prompts: site scenes
> **Superseded:** the phone-screen shots in sections 3 and 5 proved hard for AI tools. Use `scenes-per-person.md` ("SiteBakkie scenes per person.md"): the phone is always seen from behind.


### How to use these

1. **Make the character reference first.** Generate one 9:16 image from the CHARACTER text
   (text-to-image). Use it as the reference image in every scene with her, or her face and
   clothes change between clips.
2. **One scene = one clip.** Paste GLOBAL STYLE + CHARACTER (where marked) + the scene prompt.
   Make 2–3 takes of each and keep the best.
3. **No text or app screens from the AI.** AI video scrambles text. Phone screens stay a plain
   white glow; place the real app screens (`slides/` or the frames of `sitebakkie.mp4`) on
   them in the editor.
4. **Check every take for safety mistakes before you use it.** The audience is safety
   officers and builders; they will spot a missing hard hat at once. Reject takes with: no
   hard hat or vest, no safety boots, a person inside an open trench with no shoring, a
   scaffold without guardrails, a worker under a suspended load, or a bucket over people.

### GLOBAL STYLE (paste at the start of every scene)

```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic commercial, South Africa, Garden Route coastal town (Plettenberg Bay),
a residential construction site: a double-storey house under construction with brick walls,
steel scaffolding with guardrails and toe boards, a concrete slab, sand and brick stacks.
Natural golden morning light unless stated. Warm, optimistic colour grade with deep navy
shadows and warm orange highlights. Shallow depth of field, smooth handheld documentary motion,
realistic people. Everyone on site wears a hard hat, a reflective vest and safety boots, and
the work is shown done safely. No text, no logos, no signs with writing, no captions.
```

### CHARACTER (paste into scenes 1, 2, 3, 4 and 7)

```
Naledi, a confident South African site manager in her mid-30s, short natural hair under an
orange hard hat, navy work jacket over a high-visibility orange reflective vest, dark work
trousers and brown safety boots, a modern smartphone in her hand. Friendly, capable, calm.
Her white double-cab bakkie (South African pickup truck) is parked on site, with an orange
hard hat on the dashboard.
```

*(Change the name or look if you like; keep the same text in every scene.)*

## 4. Putting it together

1. Lay down the voice-over first, then cut the picture to it.
2. **Order for the long cut (B):** Scene 1 → app title card → Scene 2 → app "say the day" +
   task sheet (or Scene 3 with the task sheet on the phone) → Scenes 4 + 5 → Scene 6a–c → app
   site board → Scene 7 → Scene 8 → app end card.
3. Keep the on-screen captions in the app style: white bold headline, orange line under it
   (Arial Black / Arial Bold), as in `slides/`.
4. Music: light, warm acoustic or soft electronic, ducked under the voice (about −18 dB while
   the voice speaks).
5. Export 1080×1920, H.264, 30 fps. Make a 1:1 crop of the app part for Facebook feeds if needed.
