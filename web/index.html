Paperwork shouldn't run the site

The Construction Regulations ask for a lot of paper: task sheets, toolbox talks, inspections, registers and appointments, all kept on site. Most sites fall behind, not because they don't care, but because the forms take hours. SiteBakkie does the writing for you.

🛑

Keep the site working

An incomplete safety file can stop work or fail a client audit. Keep every record current, on the phone, on site.

⏱

Hours back every week

Talk for 30 seconds instead of filling in forms after hours. The app writes it up; you check and sign.

📁

A file that's always ready

Monthly audit or a surprise visit: open the safety file as one page-numbered PDF, with every signature in it.

🦺

Workers who are briefed

How it works

1

Say the day

Tap the button and talk: who does what, where, and with which machines. English, Afrikaans or a mix.

2

In seconds you get the task sheet with hazards, controls and PPE from your risk assessment. The workers sign on the phone.

3

The board goes green

Every record lands in the safety file, dated, signed and sealed. The board shows what is still red.
