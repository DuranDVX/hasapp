What SiteBakkie is

A tool to record and organise construction health and safety records. It does not replace the employer, the principal contractor, the competent persons or the health and safety officer. They stay responsible under the Occupational Health and Safety Act and the Construction Regulations.

Risk assessments and AI

The AI drafts task sheets, toolbox talks, reports and risk assessment items. It may only use risk assessment items from the company's library. A competent person must approve each risk assessment item; until then the app marks it "not approved". The person who signs a record is responsible for its content.

Signatures

The company accepts the e-signature agreement in the app. Where a law requires a signature, the parties use an advanced electronic signature or sign by hand.

Records

Records cannot be changed after signing. A correction is a new record. The company can export the safety file and print records at any time.

Pilot

During the pilot the service is provided as is. Keep your own export of the safety file.
