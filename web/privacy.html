Privacy notice

DRAFT for legal review. Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

The construction company that uses SiteSafe is the responsible party for the personal information of its workers, contractors and visitors. SiteSafe is the operator: it processes the information for the company under a written agreement (POPIA sections 20 and 21).

What the app keeps

Names, ID or passport numbers, phone numbers, photos taken at signing, signatures, trade, employer, training certificates, medical fitness certificates (Annexure 3), incident details and GPS position at the time of signing.

Why

To meet the Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993 and the Construction Regulations 2014: inductions, medical fitness, training, appointments, registers, incident records and the health and safety file.

Health information

Medical fitness certificates are special personal information (section 26). The company processes them because the Construction Regulations require them (section 32(1)(f)). Only the company's logins can see them.

Where it is stored

On servers in the European Union (the Netherlands). Voice notes and text go to an AI service in the United States to draft task sheets, talks and reports. These are cross-border transfers under section 72; the service providers are bound to protect the information.

How long

As long as the law requires (for example at least three years for incident records) and as long as the company's contract needs. Then the information is deleted.

Your rights

You may ask the company what information it holds about you, ask for corrections, and complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Security

Encrypted connections, per-company access control, signed file links that expire, and a tamper-evident hash chain over every record.
